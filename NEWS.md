## Hero

1. **DRONE HITS RIYADH'S TALLEST TOWER** — Yemen's Houthi fighters
   struck the tallest tower in the Saudi capital, Riyadh, on Tuesday
   for the second time in less than a week, wounding at least 11
   people. Saudi Civil Defense posted a picture of the landmark tower
   housing the Public Investment Fund headquarters; the Saudi-led
   coalition called it a drone attack and vowed a decisive response.
   The injured: two Saudis, two Indians, three Nepalis, four Egyptians.
   Sources: Reuters, 24 Nov 2026 (via KTWB / Sowetan / Insurance
   Journal); Saudi Civil Defense / Saudi Press Agency, 24 Nov 2026.

## Grid stories

2. **HUAWEI GUILTY ON 10 COUNTS** — A federal jury in Brooklyn
   convicted Huawei and three subsidiaries on 10 of 12 criminal
   counts: bank fraud, wire fraud, sanctions violations and conspiracy
   to steal trade secrets, after a 12-week trial. The jury acquitted
   Huawei of racketeering conspiracy and conspiracy to obstruct
   justice. The case began in 2018, over business in Iran and theft
   from US tech firms (Cisco, T-Mobile, Quintel, CNEX).
   Sources: Reuters, 24 Nov 2026; AP, 24 Nov 2026; US DOJ (EDNY),
   24 Nov 2026.

3. **ZELENSKIY OWNS SHIP STRIKE** — President Volodymyr Zelenskiy
   confirmed Ukraine was responsible for Saturday's drone strike on
   the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, calling
   it a legitimate military target carrying weapons. Norway, a strong
   supporter of Kyiv, urged caution in such operations.
   Sources: Reuters, 24 Nov 2026; Bloomberg (interview late Mon
   23 Nov, published Tue 24 Nov).

4. **TRUMP HONOURS GIULIANI** — President Donald Trump awarded the
   Medal of Freedom, the nation's highest civilian honour, to former
   New York mayor Rudy Giuliani in a private Oval Office ceremony —
   the third recipient of Trump's second term.
   Sources: Reuters, 24 Nov 2026; AP, 24 Nov 2026.
